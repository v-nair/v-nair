# Hi, I'm Varsha Nair 👋

**Principal Software Developer** with 8+ years building backends that scale — and lately, backends that *think*.

I started my career in full-stack web development, progressed through senior engineering roles at product companies, and over the past two years have been specialising in **AI-powered systems**: LLM integration, agentic pipelines, and real-time streaming APIs. I hold a Post-Graduate certification in AI/ML from the University of Texas at Austin and am currently completing a Bridge Program in IT at York University, Toronto.

Right now I'm the principal engineer at **ByteForge Solutions**, where I designed the full backend architecture of [Raays](https://raays.com) — a food-delivery platform built on FastAPI microservices, PostGIS, TimescaleDB, and Kafka. I hold 12% equity for end-to-end system design and launch.

## A few things I'm proud of

- **Raays (ByteForge)** — Architected the entire backend from scratch: microservices, OAuth2/JWT auth, real-time analytics, and cloud-native infrastructure on AWS
- **Unbox Social (Icekube Media)** — Built the AI-driven influencer analytics system that drove 70% of company revenue; reduced search latency 40% with ElasticSearch + Redis
- **JSW Steel ERP (Sutra Computers)** — Delivered a custom supply chain ERP for one of India's largest steel companies; contributed to 30% revenue growth in Q1
